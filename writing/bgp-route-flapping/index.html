How I Troubleshoot BGP Route Flapping in a Production Network

BGP route flapping is rarely a single-command problem. In production, I first separate symptoms from causes: whether the route is withdrawn by the local router, rejected by policy, affected by next-hop reachability, or caused by link instability.

My usual workflow is to compare BGP neighbor state, route age, AS path changes, prefix policy, interface counters, and upstream/downstream observations. After the unstable segment is isolated, I validate the fix with route stability, traffic recovery, and monitoring data instead of relying only on CLI output.
